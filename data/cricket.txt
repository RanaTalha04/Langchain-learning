Beneath the sun, upon the ground,
The roar of thousands rises round.
A bat is raised, the bowler runs,
And cricket's battle has begun.

The ball flies fast, the stumps stand still,
Each player waits with iron will.
A cover drive, a perfect shot,
A single run, then maybe not.

The bowler swings, the batsman plays,
The fielders chase through endless days.
A catch is taken, crowds explode,
As victory moves from road to road.

Yet more than runs, and more than fame,
Cricket is played for love of the game.
Through every loss and every win,
The spirit of cricket lives within.

And when the final ball is bowled,
The memories remain, forever told.
For on that field, beneath the sky,
Cricket teaches us to dream, to try.
